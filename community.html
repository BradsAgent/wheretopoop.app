Community Guidelines

Where to Poop is for practical, respectful restroom information. Keep listings and reviews helpful, accurate, and appropriate for a broad audience.

Allowed

Helpful cleanliness ratings, supply details, accessibility notes, hours or access updates, and brief comments about public or semi-public restroom experiences.

Not Allowed

Private homes or personal addresses, harassment, threats, hate speech, sexual content, graphic content, spam, fake listings, personal information, or content intended to embarrass or target someone.

Reports and Moderation

Users can report listings and comments. Administrators may hide, edit, or remove content, block abusive contributors, and preserve moderation records needed to keep the service useful.

No Photos

The MVP does not allow restroom photos.

Contact

To report a concern outside the app, contact support@wheretopoop.app.
