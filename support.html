Common Requests

Use the app to report inaccurate restroom details, closed locations, duplicate listings, or inappropriate comments. For account deletion or privacy requests, email support with the subject line "Data Request".

Response Goal

Moderation and support requests should receive a timely response. Our goal is to review support messages within two business days.
