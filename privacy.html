Privacy Policy

Effective date: July 19, 2026

Where to Poop is operated by BigSystems LLC. This policy explains the information the app collects, how it is used, and how to contact us.

Information We Collect

The app may collect account information, restroom ratings, comments, reports, saved restrooms, and approximate search areas if you choose to save them. The app may use your current location to show nearby results, but it is not intended to store a history of your live location or movement.

How We Use Information

We use information to show nearby restroom listings, calculate community ratings, process reports, moderate user-submitted content, prevent abuse, and respond to support requests.

User-Generated Content

Ratings, comments, restroom listings, and reports may be reviewed by administrators. We may remove content that includes private addresses, abusive language, spam, unsafe content, or inaccurate listing information.

Location

Location access is used to find nearby restroom options. You can disable location access in iOS Settings. If you opt in to saved search areas, the app may store general places you searched for, not a precise movement trail.

Data Deletion

You can request deletion of account-related data by contacting support. The app also includes local data deletion controls for information stored on the device.

Contact

For privacy questions or deletion requests, contact support@wheretopoop.app.
