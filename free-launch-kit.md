## Main Positioning

Where to Poop is a free iPhone app that helps people find nearby public restrooms, compare simple cleanliness ratings, and share quick restroom updates for the next person.

## First Two Weeks

Day 1: Post to personal Facebook, LinkedIn, X/Threads, and Nextdoor.

Day 2: Ask 10 people who travel or drive often to install it and test one nearby search.

Day 3: Share in 2 local groups where self-promotion is allowed.

Day 4: Contact 3 visitor centers, chambers, or local travel pages.

Day 5: Post a short screen-recording video showing map/list search.

Day 7: Ask early users for honest App Store reviews after they have tried it.

Week 2: Repeat with RV, road trip, delivery driver, and family travel communities.

## What Not To Do

- Do not buy fake installs or reviews.
- Do not offer money or rewards for positive reviews.
- Do not spam groups that forbid self-promotion.
- Do not claim the app has every restroom everywhere.
- Do not position it as medical advice.
